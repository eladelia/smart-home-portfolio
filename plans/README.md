# Ground Floor Plan

A black & white, construction-style floor plan of the public floor, built to work from.

## Files
- `floor-plan-ground-floor.pdf` – print / share (vector)
- `floor-plan-ground-floor.png` – quick preview (2× resolution)
- `floor-plan-ground-floor.svg` – editable source (open in any browser / Figma / Inkscape)
- `generate_plan.py` – regenerates all three (`python3 generate_plan.py`)

## How the geometry was derived
- **Room sizes** come from the brief's tile counts (tiles are **60 × 60 cm**):
  | # | Room | Tiles | Size | Area |
  |---|------|-------|------|------|
  | 1 | Living room | 9 × 6 | 5.40 × 3.60 m | 19.4 m² |
  | 2 | Dining | 5 × 7 | 3.00 × 4.20 m | 12.6 m² |
  | 3 | Entrance | 4 × 4 | 2.40 × 2.40 m | 5.8 m² |
  | 4 | Kitchen | 6.5 × 5 | 3.90 × 3.00 m | 11.7 m² |
  | 5 | Living 2 | ~3 × 2.5 | ~1.80 × 1.50 m | ~2.7 m² |
- **Layout & adjacencies** are traced from the **Dreame Aqua 10** vacuum map, the aerial photo, and interior photos. Room numbers match the map badges.

## Fixed features shown
- **Garden + terrace** off the living room (north), **opposite the entrance**.
- **Staircase** to the 1st floor in the living room (indicative position — black railing / dark treads).
- **Divider wall** (the key focal wall): black shelving + guitar on the living side, iPad candidate, dining beyond; freestanding white column at its end.
- **TV wall** (clean wall, west), living-room **vitrine + 2 narrow windows** on the terrace facade.
- **Guest WC** (#5) — enclosed room with door, toilet + basin.
- **Cream U-kitchen** with corner window and peninsula; **dining** window on the east wall.

## Drawing conventions
- **Solid thick line** = exterior wall or the central **divider wall** (the black-shelving / iPad wall).
- **Dashed line** = open-plan zone boundary (no physical wall) — reflects the open public space.
- **Three thin parallel lines** = window. **Arc** = door swing.
- Light grid = 60 cm tile module.

## Indicative items (verify / adjust on site)
Door and window positions, the kitchen counter, TV wall, divider wall and the garden outline
are placed from the brief and are **best-guess**. Treat this as a schematic working drawing,
not a construction document — confirm every measurement on site before ordering or building.
